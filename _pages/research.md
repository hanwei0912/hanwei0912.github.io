The Dependable AI Systems (DAIS) Group develops the foundations of trustworthy AI and dependable foundation models.

Our goal is to make AI systems:
- Interpretable
- Grounded
- Evidence-based
- Verifiable
- Reliable
- Robustness
through semantic representations and reasoning mechanisms.

### Research Themes
- Dependable Foundation Models
- Explainable and Interpretable AI
- Grounded AI and Evidence Generation
- AI Verification and Assurance
- AI with Software Engineering
