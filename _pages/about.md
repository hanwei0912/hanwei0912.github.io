Hi! Welcome to my homepage.

I am the leader of the Dependable AI Systems (DAIS) Group at the Chair of Dependable Systems and Software, [Saarland University](https://www.uni-saarland.de/en/home.html). 

My research focuses on building the scientific foundations of **dependable and trustworthy foundation models**. We investigate how AI systems can become interpretable, grounded, verifiable, and evidence-driven through semantic representations and reasoning mechanisms.

The long-term vision of our group is to transform foundation models from black-box predictors into systems whose behavior can be understood, validated, and trusted in high-stakes environments.

Our work spans:
- Dependable AI
- Foundation Models
- Explainable AI
- Semantic Representations
- Grounding and Evidence Generation
- AI Verification
- AI with Software Engineering

We welcome collaborations with researchers, industrial partners, and motivated students interested in shaping the future of trustworthy AI.
