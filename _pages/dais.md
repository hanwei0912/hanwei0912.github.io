The DAIS Group investigates the scientific foundations of dependable and trustworthy AI.

Our vision is to transform foundation models from black-box predictors into systems whose behavior can be understood, validated, and trusted.

## Misson

Building interpretable, grounded, evidence-based, and verifiable AI systems.

## Research Areas

- Trustworthy AI
- Explainable AI
- AI Safety
- Foundation Models
- Semantic Representations
- AI Verification
